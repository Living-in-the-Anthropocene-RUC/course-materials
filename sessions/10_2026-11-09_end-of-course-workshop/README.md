## About this session

The closing workshop, on Anthropocene pedagogy. What analytical tools did the course provide, and
what was missing? This is a structured co-evaluation, and the students design it as much as attend
it. Recommendations from this session feed into the case for a mandatory climate literacy course at
RUC, so what is said here has a life after the course ends.
