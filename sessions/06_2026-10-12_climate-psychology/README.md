# Session 6: Climate psychology: From value-action gap to ecological co-development

**Monday 12 October 2026**

Lecturer: Niklas Chimirri (IMT)

## About this session

Climate psychology: the gap between what people know and what people do, and what happens when you
stop framing that gap as an individual failing. Denial and disavowal, eco-anxiety, and ecological
co-development.
