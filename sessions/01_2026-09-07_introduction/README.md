# Session 1: Introduction: What is the Anthropocene, and what do we make of it?

**Monday 7 September 2026**

Lecturer: Laura Horn (ISE)

## What happened

Laura opened with the Anthropocene as a contested concept and the Great Acceleration data
(Steffen et al. 2015), then handed over to Freja Engelbrekt to make the student case for why RUC
needs this course. After the break came discussion and course householding.

Roughly 40 people were in the room, including students from outside the elective. When the
participants compared backgrounds it turned out that natural science students were sitting alongside
social science students, so the course is interdisciplinary in practice and not only on paper. The
students proposed pairing up across backgrounds for discussion exercises in later sessions.
