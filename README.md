# Living in the Anthropocene: Analysis, Critique, Action

Slides, readings, and materials from the interdisciplinary sustainability course at Roskilde
University, autumn 2026.

Whatever you study, you study it in the Anthropocene. This course is an open seminar series of 10
sessions taught by lecturers from all four RUC institutes (ISE, IMT, IKH, INM). It treats the
Anthropocene as three things at once: a geological claim, a political concept, and a cultural
condition. It asks what it means to be climate literate when the crisis is material, political,
affective, and epistemological at the same time.

The course is a 5 ECTS Sambach elective, and it is also a pilot for a mandatory climate literacy
module at RUC. That pilot only works if people outside the elective can follow along, which is why
this repository exists. Moodle reaches the registered students. This reaches everyone else.

**Any student can attend any session. No registration needed. Snacks and drinks included.**

## Where the readings are

Each session folder has a `readings.md` listing that session's required and recommended reading with
a DOI or a stable link.

We cite readings here rather than posting the PDFs. Most journal articles are under publisher
copyright, and this repository is public, so redistributing the PDFs is not ours to do. RUC students
reach the articles through the university library, and registered students also find the licensed
PDFs on the Moodle page. Openly licensed readings are the exception and can live here in full.

Background reading for the whole course is *The Handbook of the Anthropocene* (Springer, 2023).
Bring your own books, papers, and novels to the sessions.

## For registered students only

Examination is a 7 day individual written take-home assignment, 12,000 to 16,800 characters
including spaces, 18 to 25 January 2027. Reexam 15 to 22 February 2027. The teaching team sets the
instructions. Everything about the exam comes through Moodle, not through this repository.
