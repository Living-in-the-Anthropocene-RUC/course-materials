# The red thread

Ten sessions, four institutes, and a different discipline talking every Monday. The thread that runs
through all of them is a set of questions we put to every perspective the course presents. Laura
introduced them in session 1. Bring them to every session, including the ones taught by people who
sound certain.

### Focus

What is the problem or challenge, according to this perspective or discipline? What is left out?

### Agency

Who caused this? Who can do something about it? What is stopping them? What would it take?

### Ways of knowing

How do we know? Which concepts work for whom? Who counts, and whose knowledge is trusted? Whose
voices are heard? How do we learn, and what would change our minds?

---

The list is open. Session 1 ended by asking the students whether anything was missing, and session 10
is a structured co-evaluation of the whole course, so the thread is meant to be argued with rather
than memorized.
